`ROADMAP.md` records this decision under _Architecture that is already decided_.

The old workspace counts also included a playground package.
This project no longer builds that package.

A documented count becomes stale after the source changes.
Use it to identify a large structural change, such as a halved suite or a package that stops building.
Do not use it as a required number.
